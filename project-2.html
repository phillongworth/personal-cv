Overview

I manage the development and delivery of the Kirklees Healthy Working Life Programme for the Kirklees Health and Care Partnership, working part-time alongside the Partnership's wider health and wellbeing priorities.

The programme builds on work I have led across health, care and wellbeing in Kirklees since 2017, including the Kirklees Health and Wellbeing Strategy and the partnership arrangements that support it.
