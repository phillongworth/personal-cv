Kirklees Healthy Working Life Programme

Healthy Working Life Programme Manager, Kirklees Health and Care Partnership · 2025 – present

Overview

I manage the Kirklees part of Healthy Working Life, a West Yorkshire programme run by the Combined Authority and the NHS. It helps people whose health makes it hard to find or keep a job, and works with employers on healthier workplaces. In Kirklees that means a mix of local schemes, from 12-week fitness programmes to employment navigators and community grants. My job is supporting those schemes to deliver, tracking how they're doing, and planning the next phase.

More than 1,000 people in Kirklees have taken part so far.

The wider programme

Across West Yorkshire, about 101,000 people can't work because of a health condition. Healthy Working Life is a joint programme of the West Yorkshire Combined Authority and NHS West Yorkshire Integrated Care Board, set up to help people stay in or return to work, and to narrow health inequalities in the communities that need it most. It supports the Government's "Getting Britain Working" plans from November 2024.

  • £37 million for West Yorkshire as a whole, made up of Connect to Work (£16m), the NHS Health and Growth Accelerator (£11m) and the Combined Authority's Trailblazer (£10m)
  • An aim for the NHS-led part to help 1,300 people who are, or are at risk of becoming, economically inactive because of ill health to return to or stay in work
  • A longer-term vision of the healthiest residents and workforce in England by 2040, built on joined-up local systems, support designed around the person, and working with employers

What it looks like in Kirklees

Kirklees runs a range of local schemes, delivered by partners who know their communities:

  • C+K Careers: confidence and job skills for 16 to 25 year olds with health or mental health challenges
  • Fitness for Work (Kirklees Active Leisure): a 12-week programme of structured exercise and social activity
  • Wellness to Work (Locala): employment navigators and health professionals building action plans that cover both health and work goals
  • The LAB Project (Proper Job Theatre Company): group sessions using creative activity and peer support for people with mental ill health
  • Wellness Works (Third Sector Leaders Kirklees): personalised support and activities, delivered through local community partners
  • Get Set Goal (University of Huddersfield): healthcare students giving health checks and lifestyle advice to employees in workplaces

There's also a community grants programme, run by the Council's Employment and Skills Service with Third Sector Leaders, which backs community projects that help economically inactive residents build confidence and take early steps towards training or work.
