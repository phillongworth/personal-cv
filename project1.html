Kirklees Health and Wellbeing Strategy

Senior Manager, Kirklees Council · 2017 – 2023

Overview

The Kirklees Health and Wellbeing Strategy set the shared direction for how the Council, the NHS and voluntary sector partners would work together to improve health outcomes across the borough and narrow the gap between its communities.

I led the development and implementation of the strategy, working across the organisations that would have to deliver it rather than for any single one of them.

What the work involved

  • Leading the development and implementation of the Kirklees Health and Wellbeing Strategy, from evidence base through to delivery arrangements
  • Leading the Kirklees Joint Strategic Assessment — the shared analysis of local needs that gave the strategy its evidence base and gave partners a common starting point
  • Building the strategic partnerships and governance systems needed to hold the strategy together, including the Kirklees Health and Wellbeing Board and the borough's contribution to the West Yorkshire Integrated Care System
  • Managing the £50m+ Better Care Fund pooled budget, the principal financial mechanism for integrating health and social care locally
  • Leading on social care workforce strategy and on learning and organisational development across Kirklees

Governance

I chaired the Kirklees Health and Wellbeing Board, the statutory forum bringing together the Council, NHS partners and the voluntary and community sector. Chairing the body responsible for the strategy meant carrying it from development into accountability for its delivery.

What made it difficult

Strategies of this kind have no single line of command behind them. Delivery depended on organisations with their own boards, budgets and statutory duties choosing to align — so the work was as much about building durable partnerships and governance that would outlast any individual post-holder as it was about the document itself.

The period also spanned the pandemic, during which I led the West Yorkshire PPE response and the Covid vaccination programme for social care staff in Kirklees.
